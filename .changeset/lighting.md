Star lighting and comet tails. With `lightFromStar`, planets, moons, ring discs and atmospheres are now shaded from the star's true direction, with analytic eclipse shadows (umbra and penumbra, a moon's on its planet and a planet's on its moons) and ring shadows (a planet's on its rings and theirs on it). These are computed in float64 from true km positions, so they are correct at every zoom under the floating origin. Atmosphere rims are brighter on the day side. The new `starLighting` prop sets `nightSideBrightness` (default 0.04), `ringShadows` and `eclipseShadows` (default true). Comets orbiting the star get a real ion tail and a curved dust tail that grow towards perihelion and vanish beyond ~5 AU (new `cometTails` prop, default true). The new `initialDate` prop starts the clock at a chosen date. New core exports: `resolveAllTruePositionsKm`, `trueOffsetKm`, `sphereShadowFactor`, `ringShadowTransmission`, `discOverlapFraction`, `rankShadowCasters`, `cometTailGeometry`, `cometActivity`, `ionTailDirection`, `ionTailLengthKm`, `ionTailAberration`; and from `orbital-engine/react`, `DEFAULT_STAR_LIGHTING` and `StarLightingSettings`.
